Galway Bus shows live bus times for Galway city.

- Real-time departures for your saved stops
- Bus stops near you, on a map
- Live on-map bus tracking with a journey timeline
- Network-wide stop search
- Scan a stop's number with your camera to jump straight to its departures

Schedule and live-arrival data come from GTFS and GTFS-Realtime feeds.

Galway Bus is an open-source sample app built with Compose Multiplatform, sharing a single UI across iOS, Android and Desktop. Source: https://github.com/joreilly/GalwayBus
